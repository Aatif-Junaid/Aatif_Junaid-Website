Cookie policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This personal portfolio does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, session replay, or social-media tracking widgets in its site code. It does not store tracking identifiers in local storage or session storage.

Necessary delivery and security

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. Depending on its security checks, it may use security cookies, for example a clearance cookie after a challenge. These serve security purposes rather than advertising. The exact security cookies and their lifetime depend on the checks Cloudflare applies to your request.

Do you need to accept cookies?

There are no optional cookies or tracking technologies in this site's implementation to accept or reject, so no optional-cookie consent banner is displayed. If optional tracking is introduced, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before it runs.

Your browser and external sites

You can inspect or remove cookies using your browser settings. Blocking security cookies may prevent a security challenge from completing. LinkedIn, GitHub, and other external destinations apply their own cookie policies after you visit them; they are links, not embedded tracking widgets here.
