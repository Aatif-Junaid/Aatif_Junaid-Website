Policies · Accessibility

Accessibility

Last updated September 7, 2026.

I aim to make this portfolio usable with a keyboard, assistive technology, and browser zoom. This is an ongoing effort, not a certification of complete conformance.

Available features

Pages include a skip link, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, and text alternatives for meaningful images. Decorative images have empty alternatives to avoid unnecessary repetition. The site respects reduced-motion preferences. The timeline animation on the home page has a pause control in the footer, and the demonstration video has native playback controls.

Alternative formats and feedback

If a page, image, video, or PDF is difficult to use, email aatif.recruitment@gmail.com with the page address and the information you need. You can request the resume or work examples in a text format. Include your browser or assistive technology if helpful; no medical information is needed.
