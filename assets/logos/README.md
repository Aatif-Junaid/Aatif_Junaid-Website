# AM mark

The AM mark is the approved Aatif Junaid Mulla monogram. The A is the outer frame and the M sits inside it with intentional negative space. Do not connect the M to the frame or thicken its lines.

## Files

| File | Use it for | Do not use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `am-mark-glossy.svg` | Website headers, hero surfaces, presentations, social graphics, and other large digital placements | Very small marks, black-and-white print, stamps, or laser engraving |
| `am-mark-flat.svg` | Documents, black-and-white print, stamps, laser engraving, embroidery, and small digital placements | Surfaces where the glossy web finish is part of the intended visual treatment |

Both are vector SVGs with a 128 by 128 viewbox. They are resolution-independent, so they can be used at high resolution without raster upscaling.

## Approved decision

The separated-M geometry is the master mark. It was chosen over the joined M-and-frame alternative.

### Why the separated M is preferred

- Clear hierarchy: the A provides the structural frame and the M is a readable inner element.
- Small-size legibility: the open space keeps the letterforms from merging in a header, document footer, or stamp.
- Better fit: it preserves the refined blue depth and restrained glow used on the website.
- Reusable: the same geometry works with the glossy web treatment and the flat production treatment.

### Tradeoffs

- The mark is not intended to be a literal uninterrupted pen stroke.
- Its inner gap is essential. Do not close it, add extra joins, or increase the M stroke width.
- The glossy version uses gradients and filters by design. Use the flat version wherever those effects may reproduce poorly.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space equal to at least one-quarter of the mark height on every side.
- Use the glossy version at 32px or larger on digital surfaces. Use the flat version below that size.
- Use the flat version at 10mm or larger in print. Test any smaller application with a physical proof first.
- Keep the 128 by 128 viewbox intact. Do not crop, rotate, stretch, recolor, or rearrange the mark.
- On light surfaces, use the blue flat mark. Where one-colour reproduction is required, recolor the complete flat SVG as a single unit only.
