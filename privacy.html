Privacy policy

Last updated September 7, 2026.

This policy explains how Aatif Junaid Mulla, based in San Francisco, California, handles information connected with this personal portfolio.

Information used to provide the site

GitHub Pages hosts this website and Cloudflare delivers and protects it. These providers receive technical information such as IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time to deliver pages, maintain security, and operate their services. Their infrastructure may process information outside your state or country.

When you contact me

Email and telephone links open your own communication application. The site has no submission form, account registration, newsletter signup, or checkout. If you contact me, I receive the contact details and message you choose to send and use them to respond and handle the conversation. Email is handled through Gmail. Please do not send sensitive personal information or confidential employer or client material.

A message is not permission to enroll you in marketing. This website does not automatically send visitor or contact information to a CRM, mailing list, or n8n workflow.

Analytics and cookies

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It runs without cookies, local storage, or fingerprinting and reports aggregate figures only. There are no advertising pixels, session replay, or tracking cookies in the site code, and fonts and images are served from this domain.

Cloudflare may set a security cookie to protect a request, for example after a challenge. That serves security, not advertising. There are no optional cookies to accept or reject, so no consent banner is shown. If optional tracking is ever added, this policy will be updated first.

I do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. There is no advertising tracking to switch off in response to Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals.

Sharing and retention

Information may be handled by the hosting, security, and email providers needed to operate the site and respond to messages, or disclosed when legally required. External links lead to independently operated websites with their own policies.

Correspondence is kept as needed to manage the conversation, maintain relevant professional records, or meet legal obligations. Provider logs and backups follow the providers' own retention arrangements. There is no single fixed retention period for all of these records.

Your choices and requests

You may ask about, request correction of, or request deletion of information you sent me by emailing aatif.recruitment@gmail.com. I may need to verify a request without collecting unnecessary information. Applicable law may provide additional rights or require retention of certain records.

Children and changes

This professional portfolio is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. Contact me if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Questions about this policy can be sent to the email address above.
