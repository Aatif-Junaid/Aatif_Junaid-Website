My approach

How I approach problems

First principles, then 5 Whys

I dig to the root of a problem before building, so the work solves the real need, not the surface symptom.

Design thinking

I work backward from how buyers decide and behave. That keeps go-to-market honest and the message landing.

Reading the market honestly

Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, and Blue Ocean help me see the market clearly, then choose where to compete and where to create new space.

Outcomes over activity

OKRs and a balanced scorecard tie the motion to financial, customer, and product results, never vanity metrics.
