I dig to the root of a problem before building, so the work solves the real need, not the surface symptom.

Porter's Five Forces, SWOT, and Blue Ocean help me see the landscape clearly, then choose where to compete and where to create new space.

Outcomes over activity

OKRs and a balanced scorecard tie the motion to financial, customer, and product results, never vanity metrics.
